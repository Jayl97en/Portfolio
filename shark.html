Shark


This was a personal project that I did out of my own curiosity and interest in 3D modeling. I wanted to create an representation of a shark jumping out of the water. I wanted to challenge myself by trying to add realistic water effects to the shark when it jumps out of the water. When the shark would leap out of the ocean, it would have a splash effect and water droplets would be seen on the shark's body. I used Blender to model the shark and create the render. The shark was modeled using reference images of sharks. The model was created by extruding and shaping a cube to create the body of the shark. The fins and tail were created by extruding and shaping planes.
