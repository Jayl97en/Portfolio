About Me

Hello, my name is Jaylen Lie and I am a game designer based in Toronto, Canada. I am a recent graduate of the Digital Media - Game Arts at York University. For a long time I have been passionate about video games, my first introduction to them was through Mario Kart 64 on the Nintendo 64. From there my passion for them has grown, to the point where I want to make games that bring joyful experiences to others, as they have for me. I enjoy creating games that are fun and engaging, and I am always looking for new ways to improve my skills and knowledge in the field. I enjoy the process of creating models for games, as well as animating them to bring them to life. The attention to detail and the creativity that goes into creating models and animations is something that I find really rewarding. I am excited to continue my journey in the game industry and to create games that will be enjoyed by players around the world.
